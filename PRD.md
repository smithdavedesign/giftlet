# giftlet — PRD

## Problem
Someone has a birthday, anniversary or send-off coming, often within days, and wants a gift that feels personal. A template costs $2–$5 on Etsy but leaves them to write and design it; a custom site from an Etsy seller costs about $40 and takes time; a hand-built site needs skills. Source: RESEARCH.md (Etsy love-letter listings, Kudoboard pricing).

## Target user
A consumer with a partner, parent, friend or coworker's occasion in the next 1–7 days, comfortable on a phone, with photos on hand and no design skill. Secondary: a coworker organizing a retirement or farewell page.

## Competitors & gap
Etsy template sellers ($2–$5) hand off the work; custom Etsy sellers (about $40) are slow; Kudoboard ($5.99–$19.99) is a group card; Carrd and Canva are blank-page builders. Gap: a finished, single-occasion page in about two minutes from a few answers and the buyer's photos, with funny copy and fake statistics, for $9–$19.

## MVP scope
- Five-question form (recipient name, occasion, relationship, 3–5 specific memories, tone) plus 3–12 photo uploads.
- AI-written headline, sections and playful fake statistics from the answers, with an edit step before paying.
- Two or three themes for birthday, anniversary and retirement/send-off.
- Private unlisted link with a random slug, a takedown link and a small "make one" credit on each page.
- Pay-after-preview checkout at $9 (basic) / $19 (premium theme + custom message reveal).
- Printable keepsake version of the page (print-styled PDF).
- Buyer-facing "report/takedown" handling and celebration-only rules enforced in the prompt.

## Non-goals
- No image generation, no video, no accounts or social feed.
- No pages about exes or roasts of people who did not agree.
- No custom domains in the MVP; no physical printing or fulfilment.
- No group contribution features (Kudoboard's lane).

## Success metrics
- Demand test: at least 3% of landing-page visitors from targeted posts click "make one", and at least 5 paid orders in the first 30 days at $9+; below 2 orders means pass.
- Median time from start to preview under 3 minutes.
- At least 70% of previews are paid without regenerating more than twice.
- At least 10% of recipients open the "make one" credit link (to test the viral claim).
- Refund/takedown requests under 5% of orders.
